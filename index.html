Simple. Smart. Accessible.

Three steps from printed page to spoken words. No phone, no screen, no setup each time.

  1. 1

    Place your document

    Slide a book page, letter or label under the reader. It lines up on its own.

    Works with books, mail, menus and packaging

  2. 2

    Scan the text

    SWARA reads the printed words with OCR and turns them into clean, readable text.

    Recognises printed text in multiple languages

  3. 3

    Listen

    Hear the recognised text read aloud through the speaker, right as it is scanned.

    Clear speech at a pace you can adjust
